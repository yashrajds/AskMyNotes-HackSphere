Instructions: Solve the following problems based on your linear equations and Pythagorean Theorem notes.

1. What is the slope of a line that passes through the points (2, 3) and (5, 9)?

D) 6

A) The slope
B) The x-intercept
C) The y-intercept
D) The origin

3. Which of the following describes the slope of a perfectly vertical line?
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Undefined

4. A right triangle has legs of length 5 cm and 12 cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
A) 17 cm
B) 13 cm
C) 15 cm

A) x = 2
B) x = 6

D) 64
